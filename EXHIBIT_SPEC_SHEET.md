# THE MOROCCO EQUATION — Exhibit Specification Sheet

A build-ready spec for all visuals. Each entry: chart type, exact data (from the
manuscript), axes, annotations, source line, and the argument the exhibit must make.

**Design system (apply to ALL exhibits):**
- Palette: deep navy (#1B2A4A) + one accent (e.g. amber #E0A526) + neutral grey. Max 3 colors.
- One title font, one body font. No 3D, no shadows, no clutter.
- Every exhibit gets: a number, a one-line title, a one-line subtitle (the "so what"), and a SOURCE line.
- Build in Datawrapper (cleanest, free, newsroom-grade) or Excel. Export PNG at high res.

### Exhibit 6 — Inflation vs Policy Rate

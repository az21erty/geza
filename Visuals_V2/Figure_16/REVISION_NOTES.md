# Figure 16C - Revision Notes
**Mandate:** The trajectory direction creates ambiguity - a reader cannot immediately tell
whether value added increases upward or downward. Reverse the visual logic; redesign so the
progression is immediately obvious. Goal: zero ambiguity. Provide rationale.

## Old-version assessment
- The value-added "trajectory" did not make its **direction** self-evident. Without reading the
  caption, a viewer could not tell whether moving along the chain meant *more* value or *less*.
- Ambiguous direction is fatal for this exhibit, because the entire analytical point is that OCP
  **climbs** the chain (raw -> processed = more value captured per tonne).

## New version & rationale for the redesign
The redesign encodes the message in **position, height, and direction simultaneously**, so the
"up = more value" reading is forced and redundant:

1. **Ascending staircase.** Five steps of strictly increasing height, left to right. Height is the
   single most intuitive encoding of "more"; a rising staircase cannot be misread as a decline.
2. **Left = raw, right = processed.** The x-direction maps to degree of downstream processing, the
   natural reading order (left-to-right).
3. **Redundant directional cues (defence in depth against ambiguity):**
   - a gold rising arrow ribbon sweeping from the low-left corner to the high-right corner;
   - the words **"RAW MATERIAL"** anchored at the low corner and **"MORE VALUE ADDED"** at the
     high corner;
   - arrowed axis titles ("Degree of downstream processing ->" and "Value added per tonne" with an
     upward arrow).
4. **Strategic colour.** Established stages in the green family; the emerging battery-precursor
   stage in muted gold and tagged "EMERGING", separating the proven climb from the forward bet.
5. **One hard anchor.** A callout states the validated quantitative fact - 2024 revenue
   MAD 96.99 bn, 69% from fertilizers (vs raw rock historically) - so the schematic ladder is
   tethered to a real measurement.

## Note on stage ordering
The manuscript phrases the climb loosely as "fertilizer production, then phosphoric acid, then
specialty chemicals." The chemically/industrially correct order is **rock -> phosphoric acid ->
fertilizers -> specialty**, which is also the correct order of increasing value. V2 uses the
correct order and appends the emerging **LFP battery-precursor** stage.

## Why schematic (not measured) heights
Per-tonne margins by processing stage are proprietary and not disclosed by OCP. The step heights
are therefore an explicit **relative, illustrative** ladder (labelled as such on-chart and in the
dataset), while the quantitative claim shown is the disclosed revenue mix. This keeps the figure
honest: the *direction and ordering* are factual; the *exact spacing* is illustrative.

## What is schematic (explicitly NOT measured)
- The **step heights / value-per-tonne index** are illustrative and relative. OCP does not publish
  per-stage unit margins, so no measured per-tonne series exists to chart. The figure communicates
  *ordering and direction*, both of which are factual, while clearly labelling the spacing as
  schematic (on-chart text + dataset comment).

## Judgement calls
- Stage order corrected from the manuscript's loose phrasing ("fertilizer then phosphoric acid")
  to the correct chemical/industrial sequence (acid precedes fertilizer).
- The emerging LFP-precursor stage is included because it is the explicit next rung of OCP's
  strategy; it is colour- and label-differentiated as emerging, not established.
